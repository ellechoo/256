09.03 is a collection of 254 photographs taken on September 3rd across 32 years, from 1995 to 2026. Scraped from Wikimedia Commons, the project explores photographic metadata and how it can be organized and visualized. The collection begins in 1995 because earlier photographs often lacked the metadata needed for the project.

The YEAR and DEVICE layouts are inspired by tree rings, symbolizing growth and the passage of time while referencing September 3rd as my birthday. YEAR experiments with three-dimensionality, while DEVICE translates the same structure into two dimensions.
